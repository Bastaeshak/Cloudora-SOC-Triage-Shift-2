# CLD-0208: Malicious URL detected post-delivery: docusign-verify.example

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 2 · 2026-08-26
Verdict: Duplicate · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported High)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
CLD-0208 - Malicious URL detected post-delivery

Rule "URL verdict change after delivery" fired for the Bexley/DocuSign email delivered to marcus.oje@cloudora.io at 07:44 UTC. Post-delivery sandbox detonation classified the landing page at 192.0.2.203 as credential harvesting. Gateway scope identified one recipient tenant-wide.

## Hypothesis
Malicious: The URL belongs to the credential-harvesting phishing campaign already reported by Marcus under CLD-0205, and the post-delivery sandbox verdict independently confirms the malicious landing page.

Benign: The URL could belong to a legitimate Bexley/DocuSign workflow that was incorrectly classified by the gateway sandbox.

## Evidence checked
Reviewed the alert details and correlated them with CLD-0205 and 01_reported_bexley_contract.eml.

CLD-0208 references the same 07:44 UTC message, Marcus Oje, subject, and 192.0.2.203 infrastructure investigated under CLD-0205. Sandbox detonation classified the landing page as credential harvesting and established one recipient tenant-wide.

The underlying email showed SPF fail, DKIM none, and DMARC fail and contained the credential-entry URL. Marcus reported that he did not enter credentials. Available sign-in and proxy searches found no activity involving 192.0.2.203, and the supplied proxy evidence contains no matching connection.

## Action
Close CLD-0208 as a Duplicate of CLD-0205, which remains the incident of record. CLD-0208 contributes post-delivery sandbox confirmation that 192.0.2.203 hosted a credential-harvesting page and confirms the delivery scope as one recipient tenant-wide.

Keep the investigation and remediation under CLD-0205 so the evidence trail does not fork. Recommended blocking of docusign-verify.example, 192.0.2.203, and the identified malicious URL is documented under CLD-0205. No separate remediation is required under CLD-0208.

No vCISO escalation is required based on the current scope. Re-raise/escalate the primary incident if related malicious activity is identified on another user/host, credential compromise is discovered, or client/payroll data access is identified.

ServiceNow: CLD-0205 and CLD-0208 could not be located in the available instance, so the duplicate closure and cross-reference could not be recorded there.

## Justification
07:44 UTC - The Bexley/DocuSign phishing email was delivered to Marcus Oje and reported under CLD-0205.

CLD-0205 detection path - User reporting led to analysis of the message. The email showed SPF fail, DKIM none, DMARC fail, and directed Marcus to the credential-entry page at 192.0.2.203.

CLD-0208 detection path - The email gateway later re-scored the URL after sandbox detonation and classified the same landing page at 192.0.2.203 as credential harvesting. The gateway also confirmed one recipient tenant-wide.

CLD-0205 and CLD-0208 therefore represent one underlying malicious email detected through two different paths. CLD-0208 adds sandbox confirmation and delivery scope but does not represent a separate incident or additional impact. CLD-0205 remains the incident of record, so CLD-0208 should close as a Duplicate.

## Tuning note
No tuning recommended. Both detection paths behaved as intended. Duplicate reconciliation keeps the investigation and remediation in one incident without weakening either detection.
