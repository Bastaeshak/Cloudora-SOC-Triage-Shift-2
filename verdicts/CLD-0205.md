# CLD-0205: Reported email: 'Bexley contract ready for signature'

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 2 · 2026-08-26
Verdict: True positive · Severity (my rating): Low (reported Medium)
QA review: Satisfactory

## Alert
CLD-0205 - Reported email: "Bexley contract ready for signature"

Marcus Oje in Finance reported an email received at 07:44 UTC titled "DocuSign: Bexley integration contract ready for signature" from notifications@docusign-verify.example. The email contained a link to a credential-entry page hosted at 192.0.2.203. Marcus reported that he did not enter credentials.

## Hypothesis
Malicious: An attacker impersonated DocuSign and used the active Bexley integration project as a topical lure to direct Marcus to a credential-harvesting page and steal his Cloudora credentials.

Benign: The email could be a legitimate Bexley contract notification using an authorized document-signing and authentication workflow.

## Evidence checked
Reviewed 01_reported_bexley_contract.eml. The message was received by mx.cloudora.io from mail-relay.docusign-verify.example (192.0.2.203) at 07:44:02 UTC. From and Return-Path both showed notifications@docusign-verify.example. Authentication results showed SPF fail, DKIM none, and DMARC fail. The message linked to 192.0.2.203/sign?doc=bx-2214.

Reviewed the shift handover. Bexley integration work is active this week, Bexley-themed phishing is expected, and the intel digest reports current use of lookalike e-signature domains for targeted credential lures.

Searched the available sign-in and proxy telemetry for 192.0.2.203; no matching activity was identified. The Week 1 proxy evidence also contains no connection to the phishing infrastructure.

Correlated CLD-0208, which concerns the same message, recipient, subject, and URL. The gateway's post-delivery sandbox detonation classified the landing page at 192.0.2.203 as credential harvesting and established that there was one recipient tenant-wide - Marcus Oje. Marcus reported that he did not enter credentials

## Action
Closed CLD-0205 as a True Positive phishing attempt. Thank Marcus for reporting the suspicious email, inform him that the message was confirmed malicious, advise him not to revisit the link or enter credentials, and encourage him to continue reporting suspicious messages.

Correlated CLD-0208 with this incident. CLD-0208's post-delivery sandbox analysis confirmed the landing page at 192.0.2.203 as credential harvesting and confirmed the scope as one recipient tenant-wide.

Recommend adding docusign-verify.example, 192.0.2.203, and the identified malicious URL to the appropriate email/web/perimeter block controls and preserving them as indicators for continued monitoring. No password reset or account containment is currently required because Marcus reported that he did not submit credentials and no evidence of account compromise was identified.

No vCISO escalation is required at this time because the activity is bounded to one recipient with no confirmed credential compromise or client/payroll data access. Re-raise and escalate if the same infrastructure affects another user or host, a credential submission or suspicious sign-in is identified, or client/payroll data access is discovered.

## Justification
07:44:02 UTC - mx.cloudora.io received the Bexley-themed email from mail-relay.docusign-verify.example (192.0.2.203).

Email analysis - SPF failed, DKIM was absent, and DMARC failed. The message directed Marcus to 192.0.2.203/sign?doc=bx-2214, which presented a credential-entry page.

Post-delivery confirmation - CLD-0208 independently analyzed the same message. Gateway sandbox detonation classified the landing page as credential harvesting and established the delivery scope as one recipient tenant-wide.

Impact review - Marcus reported that he did not enter credentials. Searches of the available sign-in and proxy telemetry found no activity involving 192.0.2.203, and the supplied Week 1 proxy evidence contains no connection to the identified phishing infrastructure.

The email is therefore confirmed malicious, but no successful credential theft, account compromise, or sensitive-data access was identified. The activity remains bounded to one recipient, supporting a True Positive with Low severity without immediate vCISO escalation.

## Tuning note
No tuning recommended - the user-reporting and post-delivery detection processes behaved as intended. Technical remediation of the malicious infrastructure is tracked under CLD-0208.
