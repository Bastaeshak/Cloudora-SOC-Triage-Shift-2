# CLD-0207: Outbound mail surge: marketing@cloudora.io

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 2 · 2026-08-26
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
CLD-0207 - Outbound send rate

Rule "Outbound send rate" fired after marketing@cloudora.io sent 640 external messages between 09:10 and 09:40 UTC through the connector for mailflow-platform.example. The messages contained the October product newsletter and originated from the marketing platform's IP range rather than an interactive user session.

## Hypothesis
Malicious:
The marketing account or mail connector could be compromised and being used to send a large volume of unauthorized external email.

Benign:
The activity could be an approved bulk marketing campaign sent through Cloudora's authorized marketing platform.


## Evidence checked
Reviewed helpdesk_week1.csv and the current shift handover.

Helpdesk Check - HD-5138

Purpose:
Checked whether the high-volume outbound email activity had been planned and authorized.

Result:
HD-5138 documented the October newsletter campaign for Tuesday morning through mailflow-platform.example to approximately 640 recipients.

Alert Correlation:
The alert recorded 640 external messages between 09:10 and 09:40 UTC through mailflow-platform.example. The messages were uniform October product newsletters and originated from the marketing platform's connector/IP range rather than an interactive user session.

The actual newsletter .eml was not provided, so the individual message headers, links, and body could not be independently reviewed.

## Action
Close as a False Positive with Informational severity and reference HD-5138. The outbound volume, timing, platform, recipient count, and newsletter purpose match the documented marketing campaign.

No account containment or vCISO escalation is required based on the available evidence.

Re-raise if similar high-volume sending occurs outside an approved campaign window, originates from an unexpected source or interactive user session, significantly exceeds the documented volume, or contains unauthorized content.

## Justification
Before the send - HD-5138 documented that the October newsletter would be sent Tuesday morning through mailflow-platform.example to approximately 640 recipients.

09:10-09:40 UTC - marketing@cloudora.io sent 640 external messages through the documented marketing platform connector.

The observed volume of 640 messages matches the approximately 640 recipients documented in HD-5138. The content was identified as the October product newsletter, and the traffic originated from the expected marketing platform rather than a user session.

The alert correctly detected unusually high outbound email volume, but the underlying activity matches a documented and authorized marketing campaign. Therefore, the activity is a False Positive with Informational severity.


## Tuning note
Consider narrowly correlating outbound send-rate alerts with approved marketing campaigns, expected connectors, send windows, and documented recipient volumes. Do not broadly suppress high-volume email alerts because unexpected bulk sending could indicate account or connector compromise.
